Discover the journey

  1. 01

    Wake up above the jungle.

    Mist still sitting in the valley, coffee on a wooden deck, and absolutely nowhere you need to be before ten.

  2. 02

    Swim beneath hidden waterfalls.

    A short walk past the last village, down a path nobody signposts, then cold water and nobody else in it.

  3. 03

    Eat where the locals eat.

    Plastic stools, one dish done perfectly, and a recipe that is older than the road you arrived on.

  4. 04

    Watch the sun disappear into the ocean.

    The whole sky turning at once, and the very particular pleasure of having nothing at all planned afterwards.
