# Source lanes and registry

Resolve authority top-down; a lower lane never overrides lane 1 or WCAG 2.2.

1. **Local accepted artifacts** — Figma, repository UI, design system, product constraints. Binding.
2. **Standards and official docs** — technical authority.
   [WCAG 2.2](https://www.w3.org/TR/WCAG22/), [WAI-ARIA APG](https://www.w3.org/WAI/ARIA/apg/),
   [Figma help](https://help.figma.com/) and [developer docs](https://developers.figma.com/),
   [Apple HIG](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/),
   [Material 3](https://m3.material.io/), the target framework's own docs
   ([React](https://react.dev/), [Next.js](https://nextjs.org/docs), [Vue](https://vuejs.org/),
   [Nuxt](https://nuxt.com/docs), [React Native](https://reactnative.dev/), [Expo](https://docs.expo.dev/),
   [Flutter](https://docs.flutter.dev/), [SwiftUI](https://developer.apple.com/documentation/swiftui),
   [Compose](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/compose)) and the greenfield foundations named in the
   platform reference ([shadcn/ui](https://ui.shadcn.com/), [shadcn-vue](https://www.shadcn-vue.com/),
   [Reka UI](https://reka-ui.com/)).
3. **Design-system precedent and practitioner reasoning** — strong, non-binding.
   [Carbon](https://carbondesignsystem.com/), [GOV.UK](https://design-system.service.gov.uk/),
   [USWDS](https://designsystem.digital.gov/), [Polaris](https://polaris.shopify.com/),
   [Primer](https://primer.style/), [NN/g](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/),
   [web.dev](https://web.dev/accessibility/), and named authors' essays with shipped work.
4. **Live products and teardowns** ([UXSnaps](https://www.uxsnaps.com/), [Mobbin](https://mobbin.com/)) are
   evidence for flow, states and recovery; galleries, award sites and social posts are discovery leads to
   verify.
